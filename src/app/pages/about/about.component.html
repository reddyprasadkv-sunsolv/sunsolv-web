Who we are

Practical innovation. Meaningful progress.

SunSolv was founded on a simple belief: technology should make business clearer, stronger and more capable—not introduce unnecessary complexity.

We work with organizations at different stages of growth, helping them modernize operations, create better digital experiences and build technology foundations that can evolve with their ambitions.

Our work brings together consulting, cloud, software engineering, web and mobile development, artificial intelligence and digital growth. What connects these capabilities is a consistent focus on solving the right problem and delivering value that continues beyond launch.

Our purpose

Closing the distance between business ambition and technology execution.

Many organizations know where they want to go but struggle to translate that direction into the right systems, platforms and delivery plan.

SunSolv helps bridge that gap. We combine business understanding with technical expertise to turn complex requirements into clear priorities, practical roadmaps and dependable solutions.

We do not begin with a predetermined technology. We begin by understanding the business outcome, the people involved and the realities that will shape success.

Leadership

Leadership grounded in responsibility and delivery.

Reddy Prasad K V founded SunSolv Technologies to bring business thinking and technology execution closer together.

His approach is shaped by a practical understanding of how organizations adopt technology: the opportunities it creates, the operational challenges it must address and the trust required to make meaningful change possible.

He leads SunSolv with an emphasis on clarity, accountability and long-term client relationships. Under his direction, the company focuses on building solutions that are technically sound, commercially relevant and prepared to evolve with each client’s needs.
